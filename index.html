About Us

Rockaway’s Detailing & Gardening

We are a local detailing and gardening business serving Rockaway, NJ and nearby towns. Our goal is simple: make your car look clean, fresh, and protected while also helping your yard look neat, maintained, and ready to enjoy.

For detailing, we offer interior, exterior, deep clean, and premium packages. For gardening, we offer lawn mowing, mulch, bush trimming, weed removal, string trimming, pavement cleanup, and planting services. Every job is done with care, clear pricing, and honest communication.
